The framework

What is a HALO asset?

HALO stands for Heavy Asset, Low Obsolescence. It is the quality screen behind the Anchor Model: the test every asset must pass before capital is structured against it.

H
H

Hard to fake

Physical stores, inventory, equipment and land. You can stand in front of it. It exists in the real world, not on a spreadsheet.

A
A

Anchored to real value

Capital is structured against assets with verifiable, tangible worth: titled equipment, physical inventory, productive operations.

L
L

Low obsolescence

The asset does not become technologically stranded. People will still need food, goods and working equipment in twenty years.

O
O

Operator-grounded

Run by people who operate in the real economy: stores, farms, enterprises. Operators managing assets, not screens.
